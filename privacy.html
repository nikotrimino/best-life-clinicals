Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Best Life Clinicals ("we," "us") collects through bestlifeclinicals.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you complete our private assessment or join Best Life Insider, we collect your name, email, mobile number, state, age range, sex, the goals you select, your preferred start date and your investment range.
  • Communication preferences. Whether you agreed to receive marketing emails or text messages.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, our hosting and service providers may automatically log information such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited.

Please don't send us detailed medical records through the website. Your physician will collect your medical history directly and securely during your consultation.

How we use your information

  • To review your assessment and contact you about a private consultation.
  • To connect you with a licensed physician and coordinate your care.
  • To send emails or texts you agreed to receive, such as new treatments, offers and educational content.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and services.
  • To comply with the law.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With the licensed physicians and clinical partners involved in your care, including licensed US pharmacies when a prescription is issued.
  • With service providers who help us run our business, such as form processing, email, text messaging and website hosting. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our patients, our team or others.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of our business, subject to this policy.

Text messages

If you opt in to text messages, you agree to receive recurring marketing and informational texts from Best Life Clinicals at the number you provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties, except service providers that deliver the messages for us.

Emails

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any email. We may still send you messages about your consultation or care.

Health information

Information shared with your physician as part of your care is kept confidential and protected as required by applicable federal and state law. We limit access to the people who need it to provide your care.

How we protect your information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as needed to provide our services, maintain medical records as required by law, resolve disputes and meet our legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. To make a request, contact us through the contact form on bestlifeclinicals.com. We will verify your request and respond as required by law.

Children

Our services are for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact form on bestlifeclinicals.com.
